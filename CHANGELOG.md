## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-23

### Added

- Per-monitor active workspace tracking.
- Current-monitor and other-monitor visual distinction.
- Underline, Typography, Dot, Grouped, and Circle indicator styles.
- Configurable always-visible workspace count and maximum workspace ID.
- Dynamic monitor group separators based on Hyprland workspace assignments.
